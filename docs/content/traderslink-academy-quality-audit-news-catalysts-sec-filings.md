## Overall Verdict

News, Catalysts And SEC Filings is ready as a major Academy course after Swing Trading Workflow. The course succeeds because it teaches users to slow down, verify source detail, compare headlines against filings, and review market reaction without treating news as a direction call.

The instructor flow is strong:

1. Open with catalyst quality and headline-versus-reaction context.
2. Teach press releases as company communication that must be read beyond the headline.
3. Teach SEC filings as source documents and then EDGAR as the verification workflow.
4. Move through current-event, financial-report, registration, prospectus, ownership, proxy, late-filing, and delisting filing types.
5. Return to common news categories: earnings, FDA, clinical trial, contract, partnership, and merger news.
6. Close with a capstone lesson on reviewing news trades by catalyst quality, chart reaction, risk, liquidity, execution, and repeated behavior patterns.

The course does not need a broad rewrite. It does need continued source discipline in later passes because SEC, EDGAR, FDA, and filing-rule details are source-sensitive.

## Major Findings

1. The course flow is strong. It moves from broad catalyst review into source verification, then into filing types, then into common news categories, then into completed-trade review.
2. The course avoids signal framing. Catalysts, filings, FDA updates, earnings, contracts, partnerships, mergers, and ownership disclosures are consistently taught as context, not buy/sell instructions.
3. The EDGAR bridge lesson is important. The course index correctly counts 37 lessons because `/academy/how-to-use-edgar-source-documents/` sits between the SEC filings hub and individual form lessons.
4. Existing visual support is strongest in the catalyst, press-release, and SEC filings hub lessons. The individual SEC form lessons are mostly text-only and should be reviewed in a later visual pass for source-document panels or filing-flow diagrams.
5. Several SEC filing module lessons had premature hard `/trader-intelligence/` links and generic product copy. Those were removed or converted into restrained review-support language.
6. Several SEC filing module objective bullets were compressed and keyword-like. Those were cleaned into readable instructor-style objectives.
7. Broad journal wording was cleaned where the lesson was not specifically about journaling. The course now uses review notes / structured review wording except for the legitimate phrase "medical journal" inside the clinical trial lesson.
8. A later Accuracy/Source Audit is still required for official SEC, EDGAR, FDA, PDUFA, clinical trial, filing deadline, and delisting/listing-rule specifics.
