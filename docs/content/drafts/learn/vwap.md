# VWAP

This lesson is part of the **Technical Indicators And Tools** course. It follows [Moving Averages](/learn/moving-averages/) and leads into [Anchored VWAP](/learn/anchored-vwap/).

VWAP stands for volume weighted average price. It is an intraday reference that blends price and volume to show the average price where shares have traded during the session.

VWAP is widely watched, especially by intraday traders. That does not make it a magic line.

## Lesson Objective

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Explain what VWAP measures.
- Use VWAP as intraday context rather than a signal.
- Review price behavior above, below, and around VWAP.
- Understand why volume, levels, and time of day matter.
- Connect VWAP to execution and reclaim review.

## Quick Definition

VWAP is the volume weighted average price for a trading session.

- It resets each session on most intraday charts.
- It gives more influence to prices where more volume traded.
- It is often used as an average-price reference.
- It can act as a widely watched intraday level.
- It does not guarantee support, resistance, or direction.

VWAP is a context line, not a trading instruction.

## What It Measures

VWAP measures the average traded price weighted by volume. It does not measure future demand, catalyst quality, float, dilution risk, or whether a trader has a valid risk plan.

Source check: VWAP is commonly calculated from cumulative price-volume divided by cumulative volume, often using intraday period data. Charting platforms may calculate VWAP from tick data or bar data, define sessions differently, and handle extended-hours data differently, so VWAP should be treated as a charting reference rather than an official price level.

## How Traders Use It As Context

Traders may use VWAP to review:

- Whether price spent more time above or below the session average.
- Whether a pullback held near VWAP or lost it.
- Whether a reclaim had volume and follow-through.
- Whether price was extended far from VWAP.
- Whether entries near VWAP had clean risk or became choppy.

## When It Can Mislead

VWAP can mislead when traders treat every touch, reclaim, or loss as meaningful. In choppy markets, price can cross VWAP repeatedly. In fast small-cap moves, VWAP may be far from the actual risk area.

## Realistic Example

A stock gaps up on news, pushes above VWAP, then pulls back. Price holds near VWAP while volume stays active and support sits nearby. VWAP helps review the pullback context. Later, price chops above and below VWAP for an hour; the level stops adding clarity.

## Common Mistakes

One common mistake is treating VWAP as guaranteed support or resistance.

Another mistake is chasing a move just because price is above VWAP.

Traders also make mistakes when they ignore nearby resistance during a VWAP reclaim.

Another mistake is using VWAP without reviewing spread, volume, or liquidity.

A final mistake is holding after VWAP is lost even though the original VWAP-based idea failed.

## Practical Checklist

- I know whether price is above, below, or chopping around VWAP.
- I checked volume and liquidity.
- I checked nearby support and resistance.
- I did not treat VWAP as a guarantee.
- I defined risk before using VWAP as context.
- I reviewed time of day.
- I checked whether VWAP actually helped the trade.

## Apply This In Review

- Was price above, below, or around VWAP before entry?
- Did VWAP act as context or as my only reason?
- Did volume support the behavior around VWAP?
- Was there nearby resistance or support?
- Did price reclaim, hold, reject, or lose VWAP?
- Did I chase after a VWAP move?
- Did I exit if the VWAP idea failed?

VWAP review should focus on behavior around the level, not blind faith in the line.

## FAQ

### What is VWAP?

VWAP is volume weighted average price, an intraday average price line weighted by trading volume.

### Is VWAP support or resistance?

VWAP can be watched as an intraday reference area, but it does not guarantee support or resistance.

### Does VWAP reset each day?

On most intraday charts, standard VWAP resets each session.

### Can price chop around VWAP?

Yes. Price can cross VWAP repeatedly, especially in choppy or low-conviction conditions.

### Is being above VWAP bullish?

It can be useful context, but it is not a standalone signal and does not guarantee continuation.

### How should VWAP be reviewed?

Review price behavior around VWAP, volume, nearby levels, liquidity, entry timing, and whether the trade followed a plan.
