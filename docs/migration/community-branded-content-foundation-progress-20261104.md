## Post-acceptance owner direction — 2026-11-04

- The owner simplified the future public model to Alert, Watchlist and Content page families. Swing becomes a detailed-research presentation template available to both Alert and Watchlist pages rather than a separate top-level family.
- Alert and Watchlist templates are intentionally non-restrictive: standard Alert, list-style Watchlist and detailed Research/Swing presets may be selected in either family.
- Content will begin with Article, Research Report, Guide/Tutorial and Brief Update presets plus bounded owner customization of labels and structured sections. Arbitrary layouts and user-added external links are excluded from the first redesigned editor.
- Image upload is explicitly deferred. The controlling plan records the approved cover/body counts, input sizes and dimensions, processing/security rules, per-post and per-community limits, draft retention, and the intended Sharp plus object-storage architecture.
- These are roadmap decisions only. The accepted preview still exposes the foundation `/swing/...` route and existing resource-link behavior until a separate implementation slice adds compatibility redirects and migrates the authoring model without losing content, stable IDs or Stats history.
- The owner clarified stable destination behavior after reviewing the existing private Admin Watchlist model. Community Alerts append newest-first cards to one stable destination. Community Watchlists edit a database-backed current ticker board at one stable destination; adding or removing a ticker changes the board rather than replacing the page.
- Community Watchlists will not use the private TraderLink AI pipeline during beta. Their template-driven add form offers **Publish to members**: selected publishes immediately and also records the ticker in management; unselected saves a management draft for later editing and publication. Optional company details and a timestamped non-live **Price when added** snapshot are captured at publication without blocking authored content when a provider is unavailable.
- The owner approved one shared multi-tenant Community application rather than a separate runtime for each server or destination. Community pages use indexed, destination-scoped database reads, short version-checked writes, bounded caching with targeted invalidation, asynchronous Discord delivery and non-live metadata capture at publication.
- The private TraderLink Admin Watchlist keeps its advanced runtime because its live data, AI and operational automation are a different product boundary. Community Watchlists do not receive continuous polling, live pricing, AI workers or per-community processes.
- Invited beta remains on the accepted single-writer SQLite architecture with measured latency, contention, event growth, cache and delivery-backlog monitoring. A PostgreSQL or queued-event migration requires evidence of sustained contention and its own plan; no unsupported capacity number is claimed.
