## Report selection and earnings announcements

- Anchor annual and interim selections to the latest applicable filing and actual report period. Use submissions report dates and source accession/context evidence, including non-calendar fiscal years and supported short fiscal periods. Instant facts use an as-of date. Duration facts keep their actual start and end and distinguish discrete-quarter, year-to-date and annual values. Classify filed annual statements in 10-K, 20-F and 40-F, and interim statements in 10-Q and qualifying foreign interim statements when supported. An earnings announcement alone never advances the filed financial period.
- Recognize US GAAP and IFRS concepts by verified metric semantics, reporting scope and units. Keep issuer totals, parent-attributable values, continuing and discontinued operations, tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive revenue, shares, and per-share data distinct. Preserve reported currency. Exact zero is supported by explicit filing evidence, including a tagged zero. Price or growth calculations require compatible operands, scope, share basis, currency, reporting window, and valid denominators.
- Apply amendments to the affected period and facts only. An exhibit-only amendment preserves the original statement. While structured facts lag, mark the target as pending and keep dated prior values visible as history. For confirmed coverage gaps, try supported official filing documents for unambiguous facts. Leave ambiguous or unsupported values unavailable. Store later comparative restatements with their own accession and disclosure timestamp for point-in-time replay.
- Identify 8-K Item 2.02 and 6-K earnings releases through form metadata and attributable exhibit evidence. Keep the announcement and filing/exhibit link even when numbers cannot be extracted reliably. Extract a preliminary figure only with a supported period, unit, currency and scope. Reconcile it with a filed statement for the matching issuer, period and measure, retaining both observations and any difference. Never let a later press or exhibit filing overwrite the original filed value by recency alone.

## Worker schedule and recovery

| Work | Schedule and completion |
| --- | --- |
| Newly tracked ticker | Queue profile, SEC bootstrap and ratios as soon as the roster revision is accepted. Reserve paced capacity for admissions and report first eligible result separately for each source. |
| Profile | Refresh every seven days with stable staggering over the week. Successful scans for an absent field resume on the normal profile schedule. |
| Filing discovery | Poll the SEC latest-filings feed on a bounded cadence, filter it to tracked issuers and supported forms, and checkpoint accepted accession work. Watch for pagination or high-volume coverage gaps. Refresh only affected issuers and deduplicate each accession. |
| Structured-fact readiness | Query the affected issuer via the paced provider adapter. Bypass an old successful facts cache when checking a newly discovered accession, while coalescing concurrent refreshes. Retry when the accession or relevant facts have not appeared. After bounded fast retries, retain pending status and continue paced reconciliation. Distinguish incomplete upstream coverage from a confirmed absent measure. |
| Nightly reconciliation | Check the SEC daily index after publication and replay a rolling overlap, checking incomplete days on the next pass. Use bounded issuer submissions reconciliation when feed or index coverage is uncertain. Advance checkpoints only after successful enumeration and durable enqueue. SEC index publication and actual times must be observed. [SEC indexing and fair access](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data) |
| Finnhub valuation and dividends | Schedule one successful qualified metrics check per tracked ticker per US trading date using the existing exchange calendar, with staggered jobs and carryover for late admissions through the same pacing. Record the scheduled trading date, actual check time and provider data date where available. A successful validated response with a missing metric follows the next normal schedule. |
| Transient provider failure | Retry only the failed source with a short initial delay, exponential backoff with jitter, and provider Retry-After precedence. Target recovery on the next eligible retry after a brief outage. Cache the source status separately from its last good values. A one-hour composite lifetime would violate this recovery requirement. |

Use durable unique job keys for ticker/provider/scheduled period or CIK/accession/task. Claim due jobs atomically with expiry and a claim token. Fence completion against expired claims and stale roster identities. Commit job completion, evidence and publication together. Persist cooldowns and cursors across restarts. Bound in-flight jobs, retries and payload sizes. Keep Finnhub pacing at least as conservative as the existing shared gateway and measure the roster against the actual request budget. Apply one aggregate SEC budget for directory, filings, submissions and facts with headroom for other VPS activity. A restart resumes pending work without an upstream burst. The SEC documents distinct dissemination and structured-data update timing and nightly bulk availability.
