CRML

This idea is based on the recent report that the U.S., Denmark and Greenland now have an agreement giving the U.S. permanent security access/control over certain security matters in Greenland and restricting hostile country strategic investments.

CRML is a mining development company heavily focused on its Tanbreez project in Greenland. Tanbreez is considered one of the largest rare earth deposits outside of China.

Although the news does not exactly directly related to CRML or rare earth minerals it is tied to rare earth minerals.

History of 2 previous runs:

First run:

Sept 29 2025
CRML announces plans to increase ownership in Tanbreez from 42% to 92.5%

Oct 6 2025
Reuters reported the US was considering directly investing in CRML. At this time CRML ownership in Tanbreez was 42%

From Oct 1st to Oct 14 2026
The stock ran from $6 to $32. As expected, they raised money during that run use towards their goal of increasing ownership in Tanbreez.

Second run:

Beginning of Jan 2026
Trump announces his interest for the USA to acquire/control Greenland.

From Jan 1 to Jan 14 2026
The stock ran from $7 to $19 reaching a high of $21.86 on Jan 26th. As expected CRML raised more money to use towards their goal of increasing ownership in Tanbreez.

In April 2026
CRML increased it's ownership in Tanbreez to 92.5%. That is a 120% increase in their ownership from the 42% they owned during the 2 previous runs.

The USA still has not invested in CRML. I assume the reason for that is because Trump turned his focus to aquiring and/or, as announced on Friday, a security agreement. This is speculative you can draw you own conclusions.

Since its first run back in Oct 2025 has become a well know stock to major investors and retail investors.

My plan:

Build position value of $10,000.

As usual I plan on scaling in and Scaling out.

Right now the tricky part is it is up 20% since Fridays news was dropped so as always in these circumstances nobody knows what will happen next...will it continue to increase or will it see a decent pullback? - nobody knows. Pullback would be great and so would a break of 8.56. Right now its currentl price of $8.12 needs to clear though resistance which is why I would like to see the $8.56 break.

Key levels (zones)

Pullback: $7.20 - $7.50.

A drop below $7.20 is where i stop adding. A drop below $6.70 is where i start to get nervous. Under $6.50 I stop out. Because I will scale in carefully stopping out under 6.50 would be loss i can easily accept.

If it doesn't pullback ($7.20 - $7.50) I would like to see a break above $8.56 and for it to hold above at least $8.00 after the break.

Basically I will buy $7.20 and above, ideally if it breaks $8.56 theres some shallow pullback for some adds.
