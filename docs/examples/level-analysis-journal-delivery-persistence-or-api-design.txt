Gate: journal_level_analysis_delivery_persistence_or_api_design

Recommended path:
Persist a normalized compact delivery summary plus the raw source payload.

Why:
The journal app needs stable queryable facts for future API/UI workflows, while
the original levels-system payload must remain available for audit, replay,
deduplication, and future schema compatibility.

Source kinds:
- single_snapshot_v1
- packaged_review_delivery

Proposed contract:
JournalLevelAnalysisDeliveryRecord stores source metadata, validation status,
rawPayloadHash, rawPayload, compactSummary, perSymbolSummary, safety flags,
limitations, quarantine reasons, and audit trail.

Proposed routes:
- POST /api/level-analysis/deliveries/validate
- POST /api/level-analysis/deliveries
- GET /api/level-analysis/deliveries/latest
- GET /api/level-analysis/deliveries/latest/symbols/[symbol]
- GET /api/admin/level-analysis/deliveries/[deliveryId]/raw

Safety:
Keep the payload facts-only. Preserve old LevelAnalysisSnapshot v1 ingestion.
Preserve raw source payloads. Quarantine malformed or unsafe payloads. Treat
15m as context-only. Do not add recommendations, buy/sell/hold wording,
coaching, grading, P/L, giveback analysis, behavior scoring, or execution
quality inference.
