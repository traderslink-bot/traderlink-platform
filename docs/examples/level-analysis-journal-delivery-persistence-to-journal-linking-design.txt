Gate: journal_level_analysis_delivery_persistence_to_journal_linking_design

Recommendation:
Use explicit JournalLevelAnalysisTradeLinkRecord records that attach accepted
delivery symbol summaries to saved trades. Do not rely on latest-symbol lookup
alone for historical journal review.

Default match policy:
- exact uppercase symbol match
- account/workspace allowed provider
- latest accepted symbol summary at or before trade end
- no future as-of attachment for historical trades
- accepted delivery only
- 15m context-only required

Future API sketch:
- POST /api/level-analysis/trade-links/resolve
- POST /api/level-analysis/trade-links
- GET /api/trades/[tradeId]/level-analysis
- DELETE /api/level-analysis/trade-links/[linkId]
- GET /api/admin/level-analysis/trade-links/[linkId]

Safety:
Keep linked facts observational. Do not add recommendations, buy/sell/hold
decisions, coaching, grading, P/L, giveback, or behavior scoring. Raw payloads
remain preserved on delivery records and are admin/debug only.
