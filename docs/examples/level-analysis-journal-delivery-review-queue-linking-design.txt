Gate: journal_level_analysis_delivery_review_queue_linking_design

Purpose:
Design how persisted level-analysis trade links appear in saved review queues
and trade-detail workflows as factual availability metadata.

Recommendation:
Add a dedicated review queue level-facts read model. Batch saved trade IDs,
join to persisted JournalLevelAnalysisTradeLinkRecord rows, and expose compact
availability state on each queue item.

Availability states:
- attached
- available_to_attach
- blocked_by_as_of_policy
- unavailable_for_symbol_provider
- quarantined_or_unsafe
- not_checked
- feature_disabled

Rules:
- Do not auto-attach links during queue reads.
- Do not change queue priority scores in the first integration.
- Do not expose raw payloads in user-facing queue or trade-detail responses.
- Keep 15m context-only.
- Keep all copy factual and availability-oriented.
- Do not add recommendation, coaching, grading, P/L, giveback, trade-advice, or behavior-scoring behavior.
