# Swing Trade Journal

A swing trade journal is a structured record of trades held across multiple sessions. It helps the trader review the original idea, risk plan, key levels, catalyst, overnight risk, and decisions made while the trade was open.

Swing trades need more than a quick entry and exit note. A trade can change over several days as news appears, volume fades, support breaks, or the trader adjusts the plan.

Because multi-session trades can gap between sessions, the journal should also record whether the risk plan assumed a fill that was not actually available.

## Lesson Objective

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Explain why swing trades need multi-session review.
- Track thesis, levels, risk, catalysts, and management decisions.
- Review whether the trade changed after entry.
- Separate planned holds from accidental holds.
- Connect swing review to overnight risk and news risk.

## Quick Definition

A swing trade journal is a review record focused on multi-session trades.

- Trade thesis.
- Entry and exit prices.
- Support and resistance levels.
- Invalidation level.
- Position size.
- Overnight risk.
- Catalyst or news context.
- Volume behavior.
- Daily management notes.
- Mistakes and lessons.

The goal is not to write a perfect essay after every trade. The goal is to create useful review data.

## Why It Matters

A swing trade can drift away from its original plan. A trader may enter for a breakout and later hold through a failed level. They may enter for a catalyst and then ignore that the news reaction faded. They may size the trade like a day trade even though it is being held overnight.

Without review, it is easy to rewrite the story after the trade is over.

## Practical Review Process

A swing trade journal should begin before entry and continue while the trade is open:

- Record why the stock is being watched.
- Write the setup, thesis, and key level.
- Define where the idea is wrong.
- Record planned hold time and overnight risk.
- Add daily notes about levels, volume, news, and thesis changes.
- Review whether management decisions followed the plan.
- Write the final lesson after exit.

The review should show how the trade evolved, not only where it opened and closed.

## Realistic Example

A trader enters a swing trade because a stock breaks above 6.00 resistance on strong volume. The pre-trade note says the 6.00 level must hold and failed breakout risk starts below 5.70.

Two days later, the stock closes at 5.85 on increased selling volume. The journal note says the breakout level is under pressure and the trader needs a reclaim or the original thesis is weakening.

That tracking helps the trader manage the trade from structure, not just emotion.

## Common Mistakes

One common mistake is journaling only after the trade is closed. That can create hindsight bias.

Another mistake is tracking only profit and loss. P&L does not explain whether the swing trade followed the thesis.

Traders also make mistakes when they do not record why the trade was taken.

Another mistake is ignoring management decisions across the hold period.

A final mistake is writing too much but reviewing too little. The journal should be useful, not just long.

## FAQ

### What is a swing trade journal?

A swing trade journal is a structured record of multi-session trades, including thesis, risk, levels, catalysts, management decisions, and lessons.

### What should I track in a swing trade journal?

Track the setup, entry, exit, support and resistance, invalidation, position size, overnight risk, news, volume, and management decisions.

### Should I journal before or after the trade?

Both. Journaling before entry captures the plan. Journaling after exit helps review whether the plan was followed.

### Why is P&L not enough for swing review?

P&L shows the result, but it does not explain whether the trade was well planned, correctly sized, or managed with discipline.

### How often should swing trades be updated?

Many traders update notes after each session or whenever new information changes the trade context.

### Can a swing journal identify mistakes?

Yes. It can show repeated issues like chasing, ignoring invalidation, oversizing, holding through news without a plan, or exiting too early.
