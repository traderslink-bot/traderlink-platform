# Candlestick Deep-Dive Lessons

Candlesticks are easier to learn when they are grouped by the behavior they show.

You do not need to memorize every candle before continuing the core course. Use this page as a candle library. Open a lesson when a specific candle appears on a chart, or when you want to study one type of candle behavior more closely.

The goal is not to collect candle names. The goal is to understand what each candle is trying to show:

* Did price test a level and close away from it?
* Did the candle show hesitation or balance?
* Did the candle expand outside a prior range?
* Did volume support the candle?
* Did intraday price shift from red to green or green to red?

If you want to stay on the main course path, continue to **Support and Resistance** after this page.

These lessons focus on candles that show pause, balance, or hesitation. The main question is whether the candle formed at a meaningful level or inside random chop.
