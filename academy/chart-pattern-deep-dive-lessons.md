# Chart Pattern Deep-Dive Lessons

Chart patterns are easier to learn when they are grouped by the behavior they describe.

You do not need to memorize every pattern before continuing the core course. Use this page as a pattern library. Open a lesson when a specific structure shows up on a chart, or when you want to study one type of price behavior more closely.

The goal is not to collect pattern names. The goal is to understand what each pattern is trying to show:

* Is price pausing after a move?
* Is pressure building near a level?
* Is price rotating inside a range?
* Is a prior move losing control?
* Did a breakout or breakdown fail?

If you want to stay on the main course path, continue to **Gap Fill Trading** after this page.

### Triangle and Compression Patterns

These patterns usually describe price tightening between levels. The main question is which side of the range price can actually hold after the break.

### Range and Channel Patterns

These patterns describe price moving between visible boundaries. The main question is whether the boundaries are clean enough to guide the chart read.

- [Rectangle Pattern](/academy/chart-patterns/rectangle-pattern/)

These patterns describe a move losing control or changing character. The main risk is seeing the reversal too early before structure actually changes.
