# Bullish Marubozu

A bullish marubozu is a green momentum candle with little or no wick.

![](/academy/images/chart-reading/candle-behavior/bullish-marubozu-shape.svg)

## At A Glance

* **Name:** Bullish Marubozu
* **Category:** Momentum And Continuation Candles
* **Type:** Bullish Momentum
* **Number of candles:** 1 candle
* **Typical context:** During an upward push, breakout, or continuation move.

## How To Identify It

* **Body:** Large green body from near the low to near the high.
* **Wicks:** Very small or no wicks.
* **Relationship:** The single candle is read by the strong close near the top of the range.

The fastest way to identify Bullish Marubozu is to start with the shape first, then check whether the surrounding candles match the context.

## Context

This candle is most useful when it appears during increasing buyer pressure or through a visible level.

Context does not replace the candle shape. It explains why the same shape can matter more in one area than another.

## What It Shows

Buyers controlled the candle from open to close.

## What To Watch Next

Watch whether price holds the upper half of the body or continues above the high.

The read weakens if price immediately falls back through the body.

## Common Confusion

Bullish marubozu is a stricter form of a strong green full body candle.

## Key Takeaway

A bullish marubozu shows clean buyer control inside one candle.

## FAQ

### How many candles form Bullish Marubozu?

Bullish Marubozu uses 1 candle.

### What should I look at first?

Start with body size, wick direction, and where the candle sits in the recent price action.

### What makes the read weaker?

The read weakens when the next candles immediately move against the pattern and fail to respect the key candle area.
