# Momentum And Continuation Candles

Momentum and continuation candles show pressure that keeps moving in the same direction, expands range, or pushes through a level.

The useful read is whether the candle or sequence shows strong body control, larger participation, or expansion beyond the prior range.

The image below is wordless on purpose. Read it from left to right, then top to bottom. It starts with strong single candles, then moves into breakout, breakdown, continuation, volume, and outside-bar expansion behavior.

## Full Body Candle

A full body candle has a large body compared with its wicks.

The candle traveled strongly from open to close. A green full body candle shows strong upward movement inside the candle. A red full body candle shows strong downward movement inside the candle.

## Marubozu

A marubozu is a strong full-body candle with little or no wick.

It is one of the clearest momentum candle shapes because the open and close are near opposite ends of the range.

## Bullish Marubozu

A bullish marubozu opens near the low and closes near the high.

The visual read is a tall green body with very small wicks.

## Bearish Marubozu

A bearish marubozu opens near the high and closes near the low.

The visual read is a tall red body with very small wicks.

## Breakout Candle

A breakout candle is a strong candle that pushes above a visible level.

The candle shape usually has a meaningful body, and the close is often near the upper part of the range.

The level matters because the behavior is about price moving through an area traders were watching.

## Breakdown Candle

A breakdown candle is a strong candle that pushes below a visible level.

The candle shape usually has a meaningful red body, and the close is often near the lower part of the range.

## Rising Three Methods

Rising three methods is a bullish continuation sequence.

It starts with a strong green candle, pauses with several smaller candles, then continues with another strong green candle.

The visual read is expansion, controlled pause, then expansion again.

## Falling Three Methods

Falling three methods is a bearish continuation sequence.

It starts with a strong red candle, pauses with several smaller candles, then continues with another strong red candle.

The visual read is selling pressure, controlled pause, then selling pressure again.

## High-Volume Green Candle

A high-volume green candle combines a strong green candle with a larger volume bar under it.

The candle shows upward movement. The volume bar shows stronger participation during that candle.

## High-Volume Red Candle

A high-volume red candle combines a strong red candle with a larger volume bar under it.

The candle shows downward movement. The volume bar shows stronger participation during that candle.

## Outside Bar As Expansion Behavior

An outside bar uses two candles.

The second candle breaks above the prior high and below the prior low. It has a wider range than the prior candle.

The visual read is range expansion.

## Candle Volume Confirmation

Candle volume confirmation belongs inside this momentum group.

Instead of treating it as a separate candle name, read it as the relationship between candle size, direction, and the volume bar underneath it.

## Key Takeaway

Momentum and continuation candles are easier to learn by looking for strong bodies, small wicks, level breaks, range expansion, and volume participation.

## FAQ

### What is a full body candle?

A full body candle has a large body compared with its wicks. It shows strong movement from open to close.

### What is a marubozu candle?

A marubozu is a full-body candle with little or no wick.

### Is an outside bar a single candle?

No. An outside bar needs a prior candle for comparison because the second candle expands beyond the prior high and low.
