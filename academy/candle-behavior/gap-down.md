# Gap Down

A gap down happens when price opens below the prior session's close.

![](/academy/images/chart-reading/candle-behavior/gap-down-shape.svg)

## At A Glance

* **Name:** Gap Down
* **Category:** Session And Gap Behavior
* **Type:** Session Behavior
* **Number of candles:** Session sequence
* **Typical context:** At the next session open below the prior close.

## How To Identify It

* **Body:** The first current-session candle starts below the prior close area.
* **Wicks:** Wicks vary. The important visual is the empty space between sessions.
* **Relationship:** The current session starts below where the prior session finished.

The fastest way to identify Gap Down is to start with the shape first, then check whether the surrounding candles match the context.

## Context

Gap down behavior depends on the prior close, current open, premarket action, and whether price stays below or reclaims the gap area.

![](/academy/images/chart-reading/candle-behavior/gap-down-context.svg)

Context does not replace the candle shape. It explains why the same shape can matter more in one area than another.

## What It Shows

The market repriced lower before regular trading continued.

## What To Watch Next

Watch whether price stays below the prior close area or moves back into the gap.

The read weakens if price immediately reclaims the gap area.

## Common Confusion

A gap down is not one candle shape. It is session placement below the prior close.

## Key Takeaway

A gap down shows the new session starting below the prior close.

## FAQ

### How many candles form Gap Down?

Gap Down uses Session sequence.

### What should I look at first?

Start with body size, wick direction, and where the candle sits in the recent price action.

### What makes the read weaker?

The read weakens when the next candles immediately move against the pattern and fail to respect the key candle area.
