# Indecision And Neutral Candles

Indecision and neutral candles show pause, balance, compression, or two-sided trading.

These candles are important because they help beginners recognize when price is no longer moving cleanly in one direction.

The image below is wordless on purpose. Read it from left to right, then top to bottom. It starts with doji variations, then moves into spinning top, high-wave, and inside-bar compression behavior.

![Wordless chart of indecision and neutral candles showing doji variations, spinning top, high-wave candle, and inside bar compression.](/academy/images/chart-reading/indecision-neutral-candles.svg)

## Standard Doji

A standard doji has an open and close that are very close together.

The candle body is tiny because price finished near where it started.

The visual read is:

* Very small body
* Open and close nearly equal
* Wicks may be short or moderate

## Long-Legged Doji

A long-legged doji also has an open and close that are very close together.

The difference is the wick size. A long-legged doji has long wicks on both sides, which shows price traveled both higher and lower before closing near the open.

## Dragonfly Doji

A dragonfly doji has a tiny body near the top of the range and a long lower wick.

It looks like a very narrow lower-rejection candle. Price moved down during the candle and returned near the open before the close.

## Gravestone Doji

A gravestone doji has a tiny body near the bottom of the range and a long upper wick.

It looks like a very narrow upper-rejection candle. Price moved up during the candle and returned near the open before the close.

## Spinning Top

A spinning top has a small body with upper and lower wicks.

It should not look like a doji. The body is small, but the open and close are not as equal as a doji.

The visual read is:

* Small real body
* Wick above the body
* Wick below the body

## High-Wave Candle

A high-wave candle has a small body and long wicks on both sides.

It is more dramatic than a spinning top because the full candle range is larger. The shape shows a wider battle between buyers and sellers inside the candle.

## Inside Bar Or Inside Candle

An inside bar is a compression behavior, not just a single candle name.

It uses two candles. The first candle is the larger mother candle. The second candle stays inside the prior high and low.

The visual read is:

* A larger candle first
* A smaller candle after it
* The second candle's high and low stay inside the first candle's range

## How To Read The Group

Indecision and neutral candles are easiest to identify by body size and range relationship.

Look for:

* Tiny body: doji family
* Small body with wicks on both sides: spinning top family
* Small body with very long wicks: high-wave behavior
* Smaller candle inside a prior candle: inside-bar compression

## Key Takeaway

Indecision and neutral candles show pause, balance, and compression. They help a trader see when the chart is tightening or when control is mixed.

## FAQ

### Is a doji the same as a spinning top?

No. A doji has an open and close that are nearly equal. A spinning top has a small body, but the open and close are usually more separated than a doji.

### Is an inside bar a single candle?

No. An inside bar needs at least two candles because the second candle is read inside the range of the first candle.

### What is the difference between a spinning top and a high-wave candle?

Both have small bodies and wicks on both sides. A high-wave candle has a much larger full range and longer wicks.
