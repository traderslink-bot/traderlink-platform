# Session And Gap Behavior

Some candle behaviors are not single candlestick patterns.

They describe how price behaves around the prior close, the current open, and the session direction.

The image below is wordless on purpose. Read it from left to right, then top to bottom. It shows gap up, gap down, gap and go, gap fill, exhaustion gap, red-to-green, and green-to-red behavior.

![Wordless chart of session and gap behavior showing gap up, gap down, gap and go, gap fill, exhaustion gap, red-to-green move, and green-to-red move.](/academy/images/chart-reading/session-gap-behavior.svg)

## Gap Up

A gap up happens when a stock opens above the prior session's close.

The visual read is a space between the prior close area and the new session's first traded area.

## Gap Down

A gap down happens when a stock opens below the prior session's close.

The visual read is a space between the prior close area and the new session's first traded area, but the new session starts lower.

## Gap And Go

A gap and go happens when price gaps and then continues in the gap direction.

For a bullish gap and go, price opens higher and keeps pushing higher. For a bearish version, price opens lower and keeps pushing lower.

The visual read is a gap followed by continuation candles instead of an immediate move back into the gap.

## Gap Fill

A gap fill happens when price moves back into the empty gap area.

The fill can be partial or full. The visual read is price returning toward the prior close area after the gap.

## Exhaustion Gap

An exhaustion gap is a late gap that stretches price away from the prior area and then loses control.

The visual read is a gap, a final extension, and then a reversal candle or sharp push back.

## Red-To-Green Move

A red-to-green move is an intraday session behavior.

Price starts below the prior close or session reference area, then moves above it.

This is not a single candle. It is a sequence where red session behavior turns into green session behavior.

## Green-To-Red Move

A green-to-red move is also an intraday session behavior.

Price starts above the prior close or session reference area, then moves below it.

This is not a single candle. It is a sequence where green session behavior turns into red session behavior.

## How To Read The Group

Session and gap behavior depends on reference areas.

Look for:

* Where the prior close was
* Where the current session opened
* Whether price continued away from the gap
* Whether price moved back into the gap
* Whether price crossed from red-to-green or green-to-red during the session

## Key Takeaway

Gap and session behaviors are sequences. They teach how price moves around prior close, session open, gap space, and intraday direction shifts.

## FAQ

### Is a red-to-green move a candlestick pattern?

No. It is session behavior where price moves from below a session reference area to above it.

### Is a green-to-red move a candlestick pattern?

No. It is session behavior where price moves from above a session reference area to below it.

### What makes a gap fill different from a gap and go?

A gap fill moves back into the gap area. A gap and go keeps moving away from the prior close after the gap.
