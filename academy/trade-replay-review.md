# Trade Replay Review

This lesson is part of the **Practice And Improvement** course. It follows [Paper Trading](/academy/paper-trading/) and leads into [Watchlist Review](/academy/watchlist-review/).

Trade replay review means replaying a chart or session one step at a time so the trader can practice decisions without seeing the full future outcome.

The value is not in pretending replay is live trading. The value is in slowing down the decision process and asking what was known at each point.

![Dark trading dashboard showing a replay review timeline with hidden future candles, pause points, volume bars, and review notes.](/academy/images/chart-reading/trade-replay-review-timeline.svg)

## Lesson Objective

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Explain what trade replay review is.
- Use pause points to reduce hindsight bias.
- Practice decisions with incomplete information.
- Review setup, risk, timing, and behavior after the replay.
- Avoid treating replay success as proof of live performance.

## Quick Definition

Trade replay review is the process of reviewing or simulating a chart one section at a time, often with future candles hidden.

It can help practice:

- Level marking.
- Opening range context.
- Setup recognition.
- Risk planning.
- Patience.
- Trade management.
- Mistake pattern recognition.

Replay is strongest when the trader writes the decision before revealing the next candles.

## How To Use Replay

A simple replay workflow:

- Choose one chart and one practice focus.
- Hide or ignore the future candles.
- Pause at decision points.
- Mark known levels and volume context.
- Write the planned response before revealing more candles.
- Reveal the next section.
- Review the decision, not only the outcome.

This makes replay more honest.

## When Replay Can Mislead

Replay can mislead when the trader already remembers what happened. It can also mislead when the trader pauses only at perfect spots that were obvious after the fact.

Another issue is missing live pressure. Replay does not recreate real-time speed, fill quality, emotion, or account risk.

Replay should train decision clarity, not create false confidence.

Replay notes should say whether the trader already knew the ticker, session, or final outcome. That keeps replay useful as practice instead of turning it into a polished hindsight story.

## Realistic Example

A trader replays a morning breakout attempt. Before the breakout candle, they pause and mark resistance, volume, spread context, and where the idea would fail. They write: "If price cannot hold above the zone, the setup is not clean."

When the next candles fail back inside the range, the review is useful even without a live trade. The trader practiced defining failure before the outcome was known.

## Common Mistakes

One common mistake is watching the whole replay like entertainment.

Another mistake is marking levels after already seeing the outcome.

Traders also make mistakes when they skip writing the decision at the pause point.

Another mistake is replaying only perfect examples.

A final mistake is treating replay performance as equal to live performance.

## Practical Checklist

- The replay has one practice focus.
- Future candles are hidden or ignored.
- Pause points are chosen before the outcome is obvious.
- Levels and risk are marked at the pause.
- A planned response is written before revealing more candles.
- The review compares the decision with the information available.
- Replay results are treated as practice, not proof.

## Apply This In Review

- Did I know the future outcome?
- Did I pause before the key candle?
- What information was available at the decision point?
- Did I define risk before revealing more price action?
- Did volume support the context or confuse it?
- Did I change my interpretation after seeing the outcome?
- What decision skill should I replay again?

## FAQ

### What is trade replay review?

Trade replay review is practicing or reviewing a chart one section at a time so decisions are made before the full outcome is visible.

### Why hide future candles?

Hiding future candles helps reduce hindsight bias and makes the decision point more realistic.

### Can replay replace live trading experience?

No. Replay can train decision clarity, but it does not recreate real fills, emotion, liquidity, or account risk.

### What should I write during replay?

Write the known context, planned risk, decision reason, and what would make the idea invalid.
