# News And Filings Path

News and filings can overwhelm traders because the information comes fast and not all headlines have the same quality. The path teaches users to slow down, read the source, understand the filing type, connect the information to price reaction, and review risk.

![Dark dashboard path map showing catalyst news, SEC filing cards, realistic chart reaction, volume, and review steps for the news and filings learning path.](/academy/images/chart-reading/academy-news-filings-path-map.svg)

## Path Objective

By the end of this path, you should be able to:

- Separate headlines from source documents.
- Understand common catalyst categories.
- Read core SEC filing types at a practical trader level.
- Recognize offering, dilution, cash runway, and share-structure context.
- Review news trades without assuming a headline guarantees direction.

## How To Use This Path

Read the catalyst lessons first if you are new to news-driven stocks. Then move into SEC filings. Filing lessons can be deeper because missing details can change how a trader understands risk.

Use the review lessons after you understand the categories. The goal is not to memorize every filing code. The goal is to know what kind of information changed, where to find it, and how the market reacted.

## Common Mistakes

One common mistake is treating a headline as the full story.

Another mistake is ignoring SEC filings because there was already a press release.

Traders also make mistakes when they treat offerings and registrations as all the same.

Another mistake is assuming positive-sounding news means the stock must move higher.

A final mistake is skipping review after the catalyst fades.

## FAQ

### What is the News And Filings Path?

It is the recommended Academy route for learning stock catalysts, press releases, SEC filings, offerings, dilution, and news-trade review.

### Why are filings important for traders?

Filings can reveal events, financing, dilution, ownership changes, financial results, and risks that may not be obvious from a headline.

### Should beginners read every filing lesson?

Beginners can start with the filing hub and major forms, then return to deeper filing lessons when those forms appear in real trade review.

### Are catalysts trading signals?

No. Catalysts create information and attention, but price reaction, volume, liquidity, levels, and risk still matter.

### Why does this path connect to small caps?

Small-cap stocks often react sharply to news and may carry financing, dilution, liquidity, and filing risks that traders need to review.

### What comes after this path?

The next useful step is trade review, where users study how their actual decisions responded to news, filings, and chart context.
