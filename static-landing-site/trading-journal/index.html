Trade Notes

Keep the reason, the decision, and the lesson with the trade.

Write down what you saw, why you acted, and what changed while the trade was open. The note stays attached to that trade instead of becoming a separate entry you have to match up later.

When you return to the ticker, its trades and executions are still beside the note. That makes it easier to remember the setup, review a decision in context, and carry a useful lesson into the next session.

The note stays with the trade.

Trade Tags

Turn the details you notice into patterns you can study.

Tag the setup you traded, how you entered and exited, the emotion you noticed, or the mistake you want to track. TradersLink keeps those details consistent enough to find again without limiting you to someone else’s vocabulary.

Add custom tags when your process needs its own language. Later, use those tags to filter trades, compare similar situations, and see which behaviours are showing up in Analytics.

Ways to Journal

Journal the way the trade calls for.

Open a compact review, keep a swing plan over several dates, or enter executions directly. Each workflow adds to the same trading record without forcing every trade through the same screen.

01 · Quick review

Review a trade without leaving the list.

Filter or sort the Trade Explorer, open the review drawer, and add context while the trade you selected is still visible. It is a compact way to journal when you already know which trade needs your attention.

02 · Swing trading

Keep the plan alive across trading days.

Record the swing plan, expected holding period, catalyst, and dated notes as the position develops. The journal keeps the original idea and each later update with the same swing trade.

03 · Execution entry

Enter the fills that make up the trade.

Add a day or swing execution with the broker-shown date, ticker, side, time, quantity, price, and fees. Accurate fills give later trade matching and analysis the details they need.
