Trade Notes

Keep the reason, the decision, and the lesson with the trade.

Write down what you saw, why you acted, and what changed while the trade was open. The note stays attached to that trade instead of becoming a separate entry you have to match up later.

When you return to the ticker, its trades and executions are still beside the note. That makes it easier to remember the setup, review a decision in context, and carry a useful lesson into the next session.

The note stays with the trade.

Ways to Journal

Journal the day or quickly review a single trade

Open a compact review, keep a swing trade plan over several dates, or enter executions directly. Each workflow adds to the same trading record without forcing every trade through the same screen.

01 · Quick review

Review a trade without leaving the list.

Filter or sort the Trade Explorer, open the review drawer, and add context while the trade you selected is still visible. It is a compact way to journal when you already know which trade needs your attention.

02 · Swing trading

Keep the plan alive across trading days.

Record the swing trade plan, expected holding period, catalyst, and dated notes as the position develops. The journal keeps the original idea and each later update with the same swing trade.

03 · Execution entry

Enter the fills that make up the trade.

Add a day trade or swing trade execution with the broker-shown date, ticker, side, time, quantity, price, and fees. Accurate fills give later trade matching and analysis the details they need.

Trading Journal FAQ

Questions you may want answers to

The best answers are inside the dashboard. Entry is free no credit card required.

What is a trading journal?

A trading journal is a record of your trades and the decisions around them. TradersLink connects executions, trades, tickers, notes, tags, trading rules, and session review so you can revisit both the result and how the trading day unfolded.

What can I record in the TradersLink trading journal?

You can record executions, completed trades, ticker-level details, daily results, trade notes, Trade Tags, Trading Rule Results, swing plans, and end-of-session notes. Calendar views keep those records organized by trading day and week.

Can I journal both day trades and swing trades?

Yes. TradersLink supports day and swing trades and is built with small cap trading in mind. Enter an execution (buy or sell) in the Daily Trade Tracker, and TradersLink will automatically detect whether it is an open trade and ask you to confirm it as an active swing trade.

Does TradersLink automatically check trading rules?

TradersLink automatically checks supported preset and custom session rules against the saved trading day. It shows whether each rule was followed or broken and keeps the result, trigger details, related trade, and available P&L context together.

Can I add trade notes and custom tags?

Yes. Notes stay attached to the trade they explain, and Trade Tags can cover setups, execution, exits, emotions, mistakes, or categories you create yourself. Those tags can later support filtering, comparison, and Analytics.

How can I enter or import trades?

You can enter day or swing executions manually, upload a broker statement, or map columns from another statement format. When a statement cannot be mapped directly, TradersLink can use AI-assisted mapping while keeping imported executions available for review.
