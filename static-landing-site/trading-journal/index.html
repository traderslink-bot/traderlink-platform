Trading Rules

Put your trading rules where the results can answer them.

Choose a preset session rule or create your own. TradersLink checks supported rules against the trading day, shows which were followed or broken, and keeps the result beside the trades that produced it.

  1. 01

    Choose the rule

    Start with a preset built around a decision you want to keep visible during the session.

  2. 02

    Set your limit

    Make the rule specific to your trading instead of relying on a note you may not revisit.

  3. 03

    Keep it active

    The selected rule stays part of the Daily Trade Tracker review from that point forward.

  4. 04

    See the result

    Review followed and broken outcomes across trading days, including the P&L connected to them.

Automatic Rule Results

See the trade behind a broken rule.

A red result is not where the review ends. Open its details to see when the rule triggered, the trade involved, and the result attached to that event.

Results by Rule then lets you step back and see whether the same rule is showing up across multiple trading days.
