Trade analysis

Trade Analyzer collects the data.

The Trade Analyzer connects each execution to the market data around it—entries, exits, candle patterns, VWAP, and EMA 9—then uses that data across trades to surface patterns in how you trade.

  1. See every entry and exit

    Every BUY and SELL is plotted directly on the chart and numbered in execution order, making it easy to follow entries, adds, partial exits, and the final exit.

  2. See detected candle patterns

    When the analyzer detects a supported candle pattern near an entry or exit, its name appears on the chart. Select the label to open details about the pattern and the candle where it occurred.

  3. Measure entries and exits

    The analyzer measures where executions occurred in relation to Session VWAP and EMA 9, along with other market observations surrounding the trade. Those measurements become data you can compare over time.

  4. Build your trading history

    One chart explains one trade. The analyzer turns what happened across many trades into usable data that can surface repeated behaviors, entry and exit tendencies, and patterns worth studying.

MFE & MAE

See how far price moved for you—and against you.

Track MFE and MAE to see how far each trade moved in your favor and against you after entry. Use it to spot poor entries, profit giveback, early exits, and whether you’re consistently taking more heat than your setups should require.

Maximum Adverse Excursion

Green-to-Red analysis

See where open profit became giveback.

Green-to-Red analysis tracks completed trades that moved into profit before falling back through breakeven.

See how much upside the trade offered, how much of that move was captured or given back by the exit, how quickly the trade first turned red, and whether price later recovered after the loss.

When green turned red

A trade that moves green and still finishes red can reveal a lot about exit discipline and profit protection. Separate trades that stayed green, recovered after turning red, or gave back the move entirely—and measure how long they held up, how quickly they recovered, and how much opportunity was lost before the final exit.

Risk-management behavior

Risk management is often what separates a good trade from a bad result. Compare how adding after a trade has peaked or reducing size before it turns red lined up with the outcome, and identify habits that may be protecting profits—or putting them at risk.

Connect candle patterns to real executions.

Patterns the analyzer can recognize

The detector identifies supported candle patterns around entries and exits, then saves those observations with the trade so the data can be compared across trading history and used to surface recurring patterns over time.

Compression and breaks

Compression records a tightening completed-candle structure. Bullish and Bearish compression breaks identify the direction when price expands out of that structure.

  • Compression
  • Bullish compression break
  • Bearish compression break

Engulfing shifts and expansion

Bullish and Bearish engulfing shifts record a completed change in candle-body control. Bullish and Bearish expansion identify a material increase in completed range and body.

  • Bullish engulfing shift
  • Bearish engulfing shift
  • Bullish expansion
  • Bearish expansion

Confirmed reversals and wick rejection

Confirmed Hammer, Confirmed Shooting Star, Lower-wick rejection, and Upper-wick rejection describe completed candles where the body and wick show a defined rejection structure.

  • Lower-wick rejection
  • Upper-wick rejection

High-volume exhaustion

Possible high-volume exhaustion records a completed candle where price movement and elevated volume meet the detector’s exhaustion conditions.

Trade Analyzer FAQ

Questions you may want answers to

The best answers are inside the dashboard. Entry is free no credit card required.

What is a trade analyzer?

A trade analyzer connects completed trades to the chart and market data around each execution. TradersLink turns entries, exits, candle observations, MFE and MAE, Green-to-Red measurements, and written analysis into results you can review across trades.

How does TradersLink analyze entries and exits?

TradersLink analyzes each saved buy and sell at the time it occurred, including distance from Session VWAP and EMA 9, relative volume, candle position, and favorable or adverse movement when supported market data is available.

What do MFE and MAE mean in trading?

Maximum Favorable Excursion shows how far price moved in your favor after entry. Maximum Adverse Excursion shows how far it moved against you. TradersLink reports both per share and as a percentage of entry price.

What does Green-to-Red analysis show?

Green-to-Red analysis reviews completed trades that moved into profit before falling through breakeven. It shows the strongest sustained opportunity, captured profit, giveback, time before turning red, recovery, and the final outcome.

Which candle patterns can TradersLink detect?

The current detector supports Compression; Bullish and Bearish compression breaks; Bullish and Bearish engulfing shifts; Bullish and Bearish expansion; Confirmed Hammer; Confirmed Shooting Star; Lower-wick and Upper-wick rejection; and Possible high-volume exhaustion.

Does Trade Analyzer tell me what trade to take?

Trade Analyzer reviews completed trades. It organizes saved executions and historical market observations so you can study your own results; it is not a live scanner or alert.

Can I open Trade Analyzer from the Daily Trade Tracker?

Yes. Open Trade Analyzer from a completed trading day to keep the chart and analysis connected to the ticker, trades, and session, or open the Analyzer separately for focused review.
