# Trader Intelligence Coaching Evidence Model

**Date:** 2026-05-10
**Status:** Supporting product/detection plan
**Use with:** `src/docs/trader-intelligence-detection-and-language-hardening-plan-2026-05-09.md`

## Purpose

Trader Intelligence has two evidence channels for finding coaching
opportunities. Future implementation should keep them separate, label them
clearly, and combine them only when the supporting data is actually present.

The app should find both:

- behaviors the trader may need to improve,
- behaviors the trader is doing well and should repeat.

The product is not just a mistake finder. It is a trade review and coaching
system that should help the trader know what to fix first and what to preserve.

## Evidence Channel 1: Execution Evidence

Execution evidence comes from the broker import and saved trade reconstruction:

- buys and sells,
- timestamps,
- shares,
- prices,
- position size over time,
- adds,
- reductions,
- full exits,
- re-entries after flat,
- open positions,
- round trips,
- same-symbol ticker stories,
- gross realized P/L,
- session, date, and hour buckets.

This channel is similar to what a normal trading journal can compute from an
execution CSV, but Trader Intelligence should turn it into plain review
language.

Execution evidence can support conclusions such as:

- kept adding before reducing risk,
- added after adverse price movement as a review prompt, not a final judgment,
- built too much size in a losing trade,
- first reduction did not take much risk off,
- trade was left open,
- repeated risky trades on one ticker,
- inconsistent position sizing,
- day trade became swing/overnight exposure when the position stayed open,
- session or same-symbol overtrading,
- strong profit protection when reductions protected the trade,
- structured execution when entries, adds, reductions, and exits stayed orderly,
- good loss containment when risk was reduced quickly after the trade weakened.

Execution evidence alone must not claim chart-specific facts. It can say what
the trader did with size and exits; it cannot say the trader bought into major
resistance, chased an extended candle, ignored volume fade, or exited before a
large continuation unless market context is attached.

## Evidence Channel 2: Market Context Evidence

Market context comes from the chart/levels system around the trade:

- support and resistance levels generated by the other app,
- candles before the entry,
- candles during the hold,
- candles after the exit,
- volume before, during, and after the trade,
- distance from key levels at entry/add/exit,
- whether price was extended, compressing, breaking down, reclaiming, or
  holding structure,
- post-exit continuation or failure.

Market context should be evaluated in three windows:

1. **Before entry**
   - Was price near support, near resistance, or extended?
   - Was volume expanding or fading?
   - Was the trader buying a cleaner location or paying up after the easier
     move?

2. **During the trade**
   - Did the trade hold structure or weaken?
   - Did adds happen near strength, near resistance, into weakness, or after
     volume faded?
   - Did reductions protect profit while the trade still had room?

3. **After exit**
   - Did price make a major continuation after the exit?
   - Did the trader sell into strength well?
   - Did the trader exit before a larger move, or did the exit avoid a fade?
   - Did a second re-entry give back profit from the first attempt?

Market context can support conclusions such as:

- bought near resistance,
- bought far from support,
- entry was stretched after the move had already run,
- entry near support had a cleaner risk location,
- added into chart weakness,
- added above resistance without enough room,
- volume faded during later attempts,
- exited too early before meaningful continuation,
- sold well near the top of the move,
- protected profit before the fade,
- left money on the table after a major post-exit continuation,
- second or third same-symbol attempt had worse chart context than the first.

## Risk And Strength Rule

Every behavior contract should be explicit about whether it is:

- a risk to reduce,
- a strength to repeat,
- a neutral review prompt,
- internal-only research.

If the app identifies something the trader did well, it should not bury that
behind mistake language. Strong behaviors should have the same product quality
as risk behaviors:

- clear label,
- what happened,
- why it helped,
- evidence source,
- repeat/preserve-first action,
- linked trades.

Examples:

- "You protected profit well" should explain the reduction sequence and whether
  price later faded.
- "You sold near the top" should require market context showing the exit was
  near the local high or before a fade.
- "You contained risk" should explain where size was reduced and how much loss
  was avoided or limited from execution evidence.

## Gating Rules

- Execution-only evidence can certify execution-sequence behaviors.
- Market-context evidence is required for support/resistance, candle, volume,
  extension, breakout/failure, and post-exit continuation claims.
- If market context is missing, show a review prompt such as
  "Review whether the entry was extended" instead of a conclusion.
- Do not say "left money on the table" unless post-exit continuation is
  measured.
- Do not say "bought resistance" unless level distance at entry is known.
- Do not say "volume faded" unless volume bars are available.
- Do not say "revenge" as an emotional fact. Use same-symbol re-entry evidence
  and cautious language.
- Do not mix round-trip accounting with ticker-story coaching. A round trip can
  be closed, while the ticker story may continue through a same-day or
  overnight re-entry.

## UI Implications

### `/coach`

Lead with the main overall coaching opportunity across saved trades. It can be
a risk to reduce or a strength to preserve.

The top focus should name:

- evidence channel: execution-only, market context, or combined,
- opportunity type: risk, strength, or review prompt,
- one fix-first or repeat-first action,
- linked evidence trades.

### `/analytics`

Analytics should let the trader self-coach by exploring both evidence channels:

- execution statistics and behavior charts,
- chart-context and level-aware sections when available,
- side-by-side risk and strength charts,
- trade lists behind each number.

### `/trades/[tradeId]`

Trade detail should show:

- execution replay,
- chart-context review when available,
- pre-entry context,
- during-trade context,
- post-exit context,
- what to fix or repeat for this trade.

### `/review`

Review queue items should say whether the item is queued because of:

- execution evidence,
- market context evidence,
- missing chart context,
- unresolved review work.

### `/progress`

Progress should track whether the user is:

- reducing a risky behavior,
- preserving a strength,
- completing reviews,
- collecting enough evidence to measure change.

## Current Implementation Status

Completed:

1. User-facing behavior contracts now carry explicit evidence-channel and
   opportunity-type fields.
2. The registry includes certified execution-only strengths as well as risks.
3. The first support/resistance market-context contracts exist and are gated by
   chart-context evidence.
4. Tests guard against unsupported market-context claims and unsafe product
   language.
5. Ticker stories now separate round-trip accounting from higher-level
   same-symbol coaching context, including open re-entries,
   day-trade-to-swing transitions, profit giveback, profit-adding re-entries,
   and repeated losing attempts.
6. Session stories now sit above ticker stories for full-day execution review.
   The first certified execution-only slice covers green-to-red sessions, many
   attempts on one ticker, high trade-count sessions, open/swing exposure,
   positive controlled sessions, and mixed session review.
7. Saved decision-review market-context insights now translate into
   product-ready chart-context findings before route consumption. These
   findings carry opportunity type, evidence channel, source, review action,
   tone, and primary-conclusion eligibility.
8. `/trades/[tradeId]`, `/review`, `/coach`, `/analytics`, `/progress`, and
   `/trades` now consume those chart-context findings for cards, queue reasons,
   counts, filters, and advanced hidden-note summaries.
9. Short-specific chart-context findings fail closed in normal routes, and
   prompt-only during-trade measurements remain review prompts.
10. Support/resistance-aware exit behavior and first-entry versus re-entry
    volume comparison are now certified when their saved evidence exists.
11. `protected_profit_before_fade` is now certified only when meaningful
    capture and measured after-exit fade evidence agree. Missing after-exit
    candles or after-exit continuation do not produce the conclusion.
12. Strength-to-repeat session stories now summarize green sessions with
    certified chart/level/volume/after-exit strengths, and `/review` plus
    `/trades/[tradeId]` can hand the user from a single trade back to the
    full-day session story.
13. Adverse-add coaching language now states the evidence boundary: execution
    data can show an add after price moved against the position, but chart or
    level context is needed before calling it a failed dip buy, repaired add,
    or added risk.

Next implementation requirements:

1. Add new market-context contracts only when candles/volume/levels are
   attached and safe to use. Do not rebuild the completed after-exit
   continuation gate, support/resistance exit family, volume comparison family,
   or protected-profit-before-fade family.
2. Route new certified conclusions through the existing product-ready
   chart-context finding bridge instead of adding route-local string maps.
3. Do not rebuild the completed strength-to-repeat session-story handoffs in
   `/review` and `/trades/[tradeId]`; extend them only if a new certified
   evidence family adds a clearer user action.
4. Keep uncertain market-context findings as review prompts or
   chart-context-waiting states.
5. Keep execution-only and market-context fallback copy clear so new traders
   understand what the app knows and what it is still waiting for.
